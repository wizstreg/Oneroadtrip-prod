☀️ Weather

Best time: April-May and September-October, temperatures 15-25°C, low rainfall. Avoid July-August (scorching heat 35-40°C on the La Mancha plateau). Cold winters with possible frost in Toledo and Cuenca.

Toledo

🛏️ Suggested base : Toledo
★★★☆☆
1

Discover the magnificent Toledo Cathedral, one of the most impressive in Spain with its blend of Gothic, Mudéjar and Renaissance styles. Don't miss the Chapel of the Kings, the famous gold Monstrance, the magnificent paintings in the Sacristy, and the Choir. An essential visit in the city of the three cultures.

⏱ 75 min
2

Explore the imposing Alcázar of Toledo, the fortress that commands the city from above. Climb one of its keeps for a spectacular panoramic bird's-eye view of Toledo. It currently houses the Castilla-La Mancha Library and the Army Museum.

⏱ 75 min
3

Visit the Santa Cruz Museum, a complex formed by the former Hospital of Santa Cruz and the Convent of Santa Fe. The Hospital building features an impressive courtyard, a marble staircase by Covarrubias, and enormous interior arches well worth contemplating.

⏱ 75 min
4

Admire El Greco's masterpiece, “The Burial of the Count of Orgaz”, in the Church of Santo Tomé. This 16th-century painting is one of the jewels of Spanish art and masterfully captures the mysticism of the era, its composition split between the earthly world and the heavenly one.

⏱ 75 min

Walk through Toledo's Jewish Quarter, one of the most charming districts in the city. Visit the Synagogue of El Tránsito, home to the Sephardic Museum, and the Santa María la Blanca Synagogue, whose filigree work and capitals amaze everyone. Discover the legacy of Jewish culture in Spain.

⏱ 60 min

Stroll through the convent district, where calm and silence reign. In some convents you can buy sweets handcrafted by the nuns. Discover the elevated passageways that the congregations used to move from one building to another unseen.

⏱ 60 min

Visit the Mosque of Cristo de la Luz, the Puerta del Sol, and the Puerta de Bisagra. At sunset, take a taxi up to the Parador de Toledo for a superb panoramic view of the city at dusk, the best reward after a day of sightseeing.

Consuegra

🛏️ Suggested base : Consuegra
★★☆☆☆
1

Behold the famous windmills of Consuegra, the giants of the ingenious gentleman of La Mancha perched on Calderico Hill. Of the thirteen original windmills, twelve have been restored, each with its own name. Visit the interior of the Bolero windmill to understand its workings and layout as a dwelling.

⏱ 75 min
2

Explore Consuegra Castle, whose origins date back to the era of the Caliphate of Córdoba. It was the headquarters of the Order of Saint John and after its destruction by Napoleonic troops was partially restored. Visit its gallery, cisterns, towers, archive hall, and chapter house.

⏱ 75 min
3

Discover the Plaza de España with its 1670 Town Hall, where the arch and clock tower stand out. In the same square you'll find the Municipal Museum with archaeological remains from the Neolithic period to the late 19th century.

⏱ 75 min

Activities

Visit the Church of Cristo de la Veracruz, whose construction began in 1550, with a longitudinal structure and Latin cross plan in Castilian-Mudéjar style. Also stroll by the Prioral Palace and the Casa de la Tercia, the former house of the Order of Saint John.

⏱ 60 min

Almagro

🛏️ Suggested base : Almagro
★★☆☆☆
1

Visit the Corral de Comedias of Almagro, the only complete and intact Golden Age theater in the world. This rectangular building with two floors of galleries was where masters like Lope de Vega, Mateo Alemán, and Tirso de Molina staged their works. Since 1977, it has been the home of the International Festival of Classical Theater.

⏱ 75 min
2

Stroll through Almagro's Plaza Mayor with its arcades, glazed galleries, and the equestrian statue of Don Diego de Almagro, conqueror of Peru and Captain General of the Kingdom of Chile. The uniformity of the three-story architectural ensemble is impressive.

⏱ 75 min
3

Explore the Maestral Palace, built by the Order of Calatrava in the 13th century on top of a small Arab castle. It was the motherhouse of this powerful order and now houses the National Theater Museum. Its Mudéjar courtyard with horseshoe arches is extraordinary.

⏱ 75 min
4

Discover the 16th-century Fugger Warehouse, built as a warehouse and offices by Emperor Charles V's bankers. The two-story courtyard with semicircular arches and the convent staircase decorated with egg-and-dart and sea wave motifs are magnificent.

⏱ 75 min

Activities

Visit the Church of San Agustín, an 18th-century Baroque building with striking frescoes on the life of Saint Augustine. The dome over the crossing is pure lacework, leaving barely a patch of surface unpainted.

⏱ 60 min

Explore the Palace of the Counts of Valdeparaíso, a 17th-century Baroque building, and the Palace of the Marquises of Torremegía from the 15th-17th centuries. Don't miss the 16th-century Hermitage of San Blas, rebuilt by the Fuggers, and the Monastery of the Concepción Bernarda.

⏱ 60 min

Campo de Criptana

🛏️ Suggested base : Campo de Criptana
★★★☆☆
1

Admire the windmills of Campo de Criptana, the very ones that inspired Cervantes in his famous novel. Of the 34 original windmills, 10 remain, and three of them preserve their original 16th-century machinery and workings. An authentic Quixotic treasure on the Sierra de los Molinos.

⏱ 75 min
2

Visit the cave house of the shepherdess Marcela, which preserves its original structure with intact walls. Discover the different rooms: bedrooms, a storeroom, a pantry, and the central room with a fireplace. A unique way of life carved into the rock of La Mancha.

⏱ 75 min
3

Explore the Sara Montiel Museum in the Culebro Windmill, dedicated to the life of this actress, singer, and film producer born in Campo de Criptana. See photographs, dresses, and even her grand piano while learning interesting anecdotes about her artistic career.

⏱ 75 min

Activities

Wander through the Albaicín neighborhood of Campo de Criptana with its narrow, cobbled, and steep streets, its houses with Arab-style roof tiles and walls painted white and indigo blue. The panoramic views of the Sierra de los Molinos from this neighborhood are superb.

⏱ 60 min

Visit the 16th-century Pósito Real, the former headquarters of the agricultural bank where loans were made in kind to farmers. It now houses the Archaeological Museum of Campo de Criptana. Don't miss the Convent Church and the Hermitage of the Veracruz from 1573.

⏱ 60 min

Cuenca

🛏️ Suggested base : Cuenca
★★★☆☆
1

Contemplate the iconic Hanging Houses of Cuenca, symbol of the city with their large cantilevered balconies over the Huécar River gorge. Inside you'll find the Museum of Spanish Abstract Art. Cross the San Pablo Bridge for the best panoramic view, both by day and by night.

⏱ 75 min
2

Visit the Cathedral of Santa María and San Julián in Cuenca, predominantly Gothic but with Romanesque and Neo-Gothic elements. Its main façade faces the Plaza Mayor and its interior houses artistic treasures of great value. One of the most distinctive cathedrals in Spain.

⏱ 75 min
3

Discover the skyscrapers of Cuenca, a group of 30 buildings that stand 4–5 stories high on one side and rise to 8–10 stories on the other, facing the Huécar gorge. A singular and striking architectural ensemble, unique in Spain.

⏱ 75 min
4

Walk along Alfonso VIII Street and go down through Las Angustias to the Hermitage of Our Lady of the Sorrows. Wander through the historic center, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, and take in the castle and Calle de los Tintes along the Huécar River.

⏱ 75 min

Visit the Ciudad Encantada, a spectacular site with rock formations sculpted over thousands of years by wind, water, and ice; allow for a round trip from Cuenca. A 20-meter monolith, the Roman Bridge, and the man's face are some of the 13 formations along the trail. Nearby is the Ventano del Diablo viewpoint overlooking the Júcar River canyon.
