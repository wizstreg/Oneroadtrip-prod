Selfoss

🛏️ Suggested base : Selfoss
★★★☆☆
1

Golden Circle day. Þingvellir first: allow for a return trip during the day from Selfoss. A UNESCO site since 2004, this is where the Althing was founded in 930 - the oldest parliament in the world. Walk through the rift between the Eurasian and North American plates, which widens by 2 cm each year.

⏱ 60 min
2

For Haukadalur, allow for a round trip during the day from Selfoss. The Strokkur geyser erupts every 5 to 10 minutes, reaching 15-20 meters high. Its neighbor Geysir - which gave the phenomenon its name - is now almost inactive. Stand with your back to the wind to avoid the scalding spray.

⏱ 40 min
3

Gullfoss: allow for a round trip during the day from Selfoss. The Hvítá river drops 32 meters in two stages into a canyon 70 meters deep. In fine weather, a permanent rainbow forms in the spray. Take the path down as close as possible to the lower waterfall.

⏱ 45 min
4

On your way back to Selfoss, detour to Kerið crater: a 3,000-year-old volcanic crater filled with a blue-green lake 14 meters deep. The contrast between the red crater rock and turquoise water is striking.

⏱ 25 min

Activities

In the evening, explore the small town of Selfoss, a convenient place to stock up. For a more exclusive soak, the Secret Lagoon in Flúðir is a natural hot spring at 38-40°C in a rural setting - Iceland's oldest swimming pool, open since 1891. Allow for a round trip during the day from Selfoss for this soak. Note: fill up in Selfoss or Hella.

Landmannalaugar

🛏️ Suggested base : Landmannalaugar
★★★★☆
1

Head into the Highlands via Route 32 then the northern F208. First stop: Hjálparfoss, a twin waterfall tumbling into a pool surrounded by basalt columns. Then Háifoss, one of Iceland's tallest waterfalls (122 m), visible from a viewpoint on the cliff edge - vertigo guaranteed.

⏱ 60 min
2

Arrive at Landmannalaugar around midday. This geothermal site in the heart of the Fjallabak Nature Reserve is surrounded by rhyolite mountains in mind-bending colors - yellow, green, pink, red, orange. The Laugahraun lava field, formed during a 1477 eruption, borders the campsite. This is the starting point for the famous Laugavegur trek (55 km to Þórsmörk).

⏱ 45 min

Activities

Bathe in Landmannalaugar's natural hot spring - a hot stream meets a cold stream at the foot of the lava field, creating a natural pool at the perfect temperature. Free and open to everyone. Note: the only accommodation is the mountain hut and campsite (no hotel). Bring your own food.

⏱ 60 min

🛏️ Suggested base : Landmannalaugar
★★★★☆
1

Hiking day. The Brennisteinsalda loop (“the sulfur wave”) is the most popular: this mountain is considered Iceland's most colorful - layers of sulfur yellow, moss green, iron red and rhyolite pink. The loop is about 6 km and takes 2-3 hours.

⏱ 120 min
2

For the more athletic, Bláhnjúkur (“blue peak”, 943 m) offers a 360° panorama of the Highlands from its summit. The ascent is steep (about 1 hour) on black and blue volcanic ash - the contrast with the rhyolite mountains below is spectacular.

⏱ 90 min

Activities

If you have a 4x4 and time, detour to Ljótipollur, a blood-red crater filled with intensely blue water - 20 minutes on the track. The contrast is surreal. In the evening, return to the hot springs to soothe your muscles after hiking.

Kirkjubaejarklaustur

🛏️ Suggested base : Kirkjubaejarklaustur
★★☆☆☆
1

Drive south via F208 then return to the Ring Road. Stop at Fjaðrárgljúfur canyon: 100 meters deep and 2 km long, carved 9,000 years ago by glacial erosion. A path runs along the rim with viewing platforms. The site had to temporarily close after a Justin Bieber music video brought too many visitors.

⏱ 45 min
2

In Kirkjubæjarklaustur (which Icelanders shorten to “Klaustur”), visit Kirkjugólf, the “church floor” - a flat surface of hexagonal basalt columns that looks like natural tiling. Medieval monks thought it was the floor of a vanished church. Systrafoss waterfall (25 m) is a short walk away.

⏱ 30 min

Activities

Klaustur is a good place to stock up - gas station, grocery store, restaurants. Make use of them, as services between here and Höfn are very scarce.

🛏️ Suggested base : Sey Isfjor Ur
★★★☆☆
1

Before leaving the region, take a detour to Hengifoss, Iceland's third-highest waterfall (128 m). Allow for a round trip during the day from Seyðisfjörður to reach the parking lot. The 27 km road between Egilsstaðir and Seyðisfjörður crosses a mountain pass before plunging toward the fjord - one of Iceland's most beautiful descents. Along the way, a series of waterfalls lines the road.

3

Seyðisfjörður is the most beautiful village in the East Fjords. At the head of the fjord, colorful houses line a rainbow-paved path leading to Bláa kirkjan, the little blue church. The village attracts artists and musicians - several galleries and workshops dot the main street. It's also the ferry arrival port from Denmark.

Húsavík is Iceland's whale-watching capital. The Whale Museum (Hvalasafnið) is Iceland's most comprehensive - it displays several complete skeletons, including that of a 25-meter blue whale. The movie Eurovision Song Contest (2020) with Will Ferrell was partly filmed here.
