☀️ Weather▾

Favor May, June, and September, with average highs of around 23 to 27 °C on Syros, a reference point for this part of the Cyclades. Avoid long walks at midday in July and August, when the meltemi often blows strongly. Allow flexibility for ferry crossings and the excursion to Achla, which depend on sea conditions.

📋 Notes▾

Arrive on Syros via its airport and finish in Athens after taking the ferry from Gavrio to Rafina, with a final night on the mainland before your flight home. Rent cars separately on Tinos and Andros, and confirm the ferries and the boat to Achla before setting your dates.

Northern Cyclades: Syros, Tinos, and Andros in 7 days

📅 7 days📍 14 stops🚗 360 km⏱ 15h15

Ermoupoli → Tinos → Volax → Pyrgos Tinos → Gavrio → Batsi → Ormos Korthiou → Andros → Achla → Rafina → Athenes

Ermoupoli → Tinos → Volax → Pyrgos Tinos → Gavrio → Batsi → Ormos Korthiou → Andros → Achla → Rafina → Athenes

In 7 days, travel between Syros, Tinos, and Andros on Northern Cyclades ferries, then continue to Athens via Rafina. The route brings together Ermoupoli and Ano Syros, the sanctuary on Tinos, the dovecotes of Tarampados, Volax, the marble workers of Pyrgos, Batsi, Ormos Korthiou, Chora, and Achla Beach. Cars are rented on the islands, while Achla is visited by boat from Chora.

Ermoupoli

Northern Cyclades: Syros, Tinos, and Andros in 7 days : 14 stops

Ermoupoli

🛏️ Suggested base : Ermoupoli
★★★★☆
1

Forget whitewashed houses for a moment: Miaouli Square presents a different vision of the Cyclades. In Ermoupoli, the grand city hall and neoclassical facades give the center the feel of a mainland city. The contrast becomes even clearer when you later compare it with the narrow streets of Ano Syros. Walk around the square before heading uphill toward the theater; public buildings are not necessarily open for unrestricted visits.

⏱ 45 min
2

At Ermoupoli’s Apollon Theater, the auditorium is as much a reason for the detour as the stage. Opened in 1864, it draws on several Italian theaters, including Milan’s La Scala, without simply copying its layout. The closely spaced boxes make that cultural ambition tangible. Choose a daytime visit if you want to study the architecture; a performance ticket offers a different experience, and rehearsals may limit access.

⏱ 45 min
3

Underfoot, the steps of Ano Syros quickly replace Ermoupoli’s sweeping vistas. Narrow passageways lead toward the Catholic Cathedral of Saint George at the top of this historic town. This Catholic presence sets the hill apart in an archipelago largely associated with Orthodox churches. Wear shoes with good traction and favor the cooler hours: the climb is part of the visit, and the narrow streets are explored on foot.

Volax

🛏️ Suggested base : Pyrgos Tinos · 13 km
★★★☆☆
1

In Volax, granite boulders seem to take up more space than the houses. The village nestles in a landscape of rounded rocks, and the same stone appears in its walls. Stay on existing paths to observe the shapes; exposed boulders heat up quickly in the sun.

⏱ 30 min

Activities

You’ll seek out the basket-weaving workshops of Volax as you wander the narrow streets, leaving the boulders behind. This craft reveals another side of the village, less dominated by stone than its landscape. If a workshop is open, ask permission before photographing the artisans’ movements and the objects being made.

Pyrgos Tinos

🛏️ Suggested base : Pyrgos Tinos
★★★☆☆
1

Understand marble before admiring the facades: the Museum of Marble Crafts in Pyrgos gives your walk context. Quarry tools, models, and machinery show the journey from a block of stone to a sculpted object. Take a look at the outdoor equipment too; plan your visit for a day other than Tuesday, the stated weekly closing day.

⏱ 75 min

Activities

You’ll follow the narrow streets of Pyrgos, looking at the door and window surrounds and carved details rather than just the facades. Marble workers still practice their craft in the village, and the workshops extend what the museum shows. Leave your car at the entrance to the village and ask before stepping into a workshop where work is underway.

Ormos Korthiou

🛏️ Suggested base : Andros · 7 km
★★★★☆
1

If the sea stays calm, Tis Grias to Pidima is worth the short detour from Ormos Korthiou. A solitary rock rises from the water off this small sand-and-pebble beach. The final approach follows a downhill trail: keep your shoes on until you reach the bottom, and skip swimming if the waves pick up.

⏱ 40 min

Activities

You’ll walk along the bay of Ormos Korthiou before getting back on the road toward Chora. The waterfront opens onto a large, wind-exposed beach that looks very different when the sea gets rough. Take this break on foot from the village, without turning every stop by the shore into a swim.

Andros

🛏️ Suggested base : Andros
★★★☆☆
1

Chora extends between two bays, with the sea as an almost constant landmark. The center of Andros occupies a rocky peninsula with the ruins of the Kastro at its far end, giving your walk a clear direction. Start at Kairi Square and follow the passageways toward the tip of the town. In strong winds, stick to sheltered viewpoints and avoid venturing onto exposed rocky areas.

⏱ 60 min
2

Shipowners behind an art museum: in Chora, the Goulandris Museum of Contemporary Art also tells the story of Andros’s connections to the wider world. Founded in 1979 by the Basil and Elise Goulandris Foundation, it offers programming that changes with the seasons. Don’t assume that works shown on an old poster are still on display. Check the exhibition and opening days for your visit before planning your day around the museum.

⏱ 90 min

Activities

For a break near Chora, choose Neimporio if you want a shore that’s easy to reach on foot. This bay borders the town center and has fishing boats as well as beach facilities, so the setting feels like an extension of town life. Walk along the water and check the planned departure point for Achla with the operator. Swimming remains an option, depending on the day’s wind.
