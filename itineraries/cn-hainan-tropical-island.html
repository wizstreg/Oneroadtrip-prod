☀️ Weather

Best period: November to April, temperatures 20-28°C, dry and sunny weather. Avoid May to October (monsoon season, oppressive heat 30-35°C, high humidity, frequent typhoons in August-September). Hainan is accessible year-round but the tropical summer is challenging.

📋 Notes

Hainan is a free port: no visa required for French citizens for up to 30 days. Avoid the typhoon season (May to October) and favour November to April (sea at 26°C, clear skies). Note: the Wenchang Space Launch Centre is off-limits to foreigners - opt for the public Yaoguang viewing platform to watch the launches.

10 stops

Haikou

🛏️ Suggested base : Haikou
★★★☆☆
1

Welcome to Haikou, capital of Hainan, your gateway to the tropical island. First stop: Qilou Street, in the historic quarter. More than 600 arcade houses in Sino-European style, built between 1820 and 1940 by overseas Chinese who returned from Singapore, Penang or Saigon. The Sipai building, the oldest, dates back to the Southern Song dynasty (12th century). A blend of Baroque, Indian, Arab and Asian architecture.

⏱ 90 min
2

Five kilometres west of downtown, don't miss Hai Rui's Tomb, honouring a Ming dynasty mandarin born in Haikou in 1514 who became a symbol of Confucian integrity. Hai Rui dared criticise Emperor Jiajing in a famous 1566 letter, was imprisoned, then rehabilitated. Mao Zedong made him an official hero. The stone mausoleum dates from 1589, restored under the Qing and again in 1996. Quiet, shaded, rarely crowded.

⏱ 60 min
3

Stop by the Temple of the Five Officials (Wugong Ci), 2 km to the south-east, which honours five mandarins exiled to Hainan under the Tang and the Song for standing up to the court. The present temple dates from 1889, in red lacquered wood, framed by century-old banyan trees. Combined with the Tomb of Hai Rui, allow half a day for the historical side.

⏱ 60 min
4

Visit the Hainan Provincial Museum, free admission, opened in 2008. You'll find 60,000 pieces covering the island's history - shipwrecks loaded with ceramics from the Maritime Silk Road, Li and Miao minority costumes, tropical forest fauna. Good context before the rest of your journey. Closed Mondays.

⏱ 90 min

Activities

Dive into Haikou cuisine at lunch. Head to Bo'ai Street for authentic Hainan noodle soup (Hainan fen) or chicken rice (Wenchang chicken rice), the island's most iconic dish, exported all the way to Singapore. Next door, a tropical fruit market: mangosteens, rambutans, dragon fruit. Also try the iced qing bu liang tea, a mix of beans, taro, and shaved ice.

⏱ 90 min

In the evening, stroll through Qilou Old Street after dark, when the arcades light up. Craft shops, café terraces, contemporary art exhibitions in former consulates. Slow atmosphere, somewhere between Macau and Singapore. Perfect for a cocktail or iced tea on a terrace.

⏱ 90 min

Haikou

🛏️ Suggested base : Haikou
★★★☆☆
1

Head for the Shishan Volcano Geopark, 15 km south-west of Haikou. There you will find more than 40 inactive craters, one of them formed about 13,000 years ago - recent on a geological scale. You can walk down into the caldera by a basalt staircase, among ferns and strangler figs. The site is listed as a UNESCO Global Geopark. Allow 2 hours on site, early in the morning before the heat sets in.

⏱ 120 min
2

Do not miss the Dongzhai Mangrove Reserve, east of Haikou (about 30 km from Haikou, a 45-minute drive): the largest mangrove forest in China, more than 3,300 hectares, more than 200 species of migratory birds. A boat trip among the aerial roots, with a pause at the observation station. A fragile ecosystem: touching the shellfish and crabs is forbidden. Very few foreign tourists, unlike the rest of the island.

⏱ 120 min
3

Holiday Beach, Haikou's urban beach, is free and open to all. You'll find blond sand, warm water (24-26°C in winter), coconut palms, street vendors selling fresh coconuts in the background. Not the island's most beautiful coastline - save Sanya for that - but ideal for a late afternoon rest before continuing your journey. Sunset over the Qiongzhou Strait.

⏱ 60 min

Activities

Book a Hainanese cooking class with a local family (via schools like Cookly or Haikou Cooking Class). On the menu: Wenchang chicken, three-sauce lamb, sago dessert. Wenchang chicken requires slow poaching, crispy skin and white meat, served with ginger-garlic sauce. It's Hainan's national dish and the basis for Singapore's famous Hainan Chicken Rice.

⏱ 180 min

Spend the evening on Haidian Island, connected to downtown by three bridges. You'll find the student and professional quarter, with rooftop bars, food trucks, and vibrant alleys. Sample Hainan coffee (Singapore style, strong, sweet, condensed milk) and watch cruise ships enter the harbour.

⏱ 120 min

Wenchang

🛏️ Suggested base : Wenchang
★★☆☆☆
1

Take the express train to Wenchang (50 min from Haikou East), birthplace of the Chinese diaspora to Southeast Asia. First stop: Dongjiao coconut grove, China's largest. Over 20 km of coastline planted with coconut palms, some 30-50 years old, alongside shaded bike paths. Free entry. Rent a bike at the entrance, allow 2 hours for the ride. Caribbean atmosphere, flip-flops and fresh coconut mandatory.

⏱ 120 min
2

Head to Longlou Town, 30 minutes by car, where the Yaoguang viewing platform lets you watch rocket launches from Wenchang Space Launch Centre - off-limits to foreigners. Inaugurated in May 2025, the platform covers 19,000 m² and accommodates up to 2,000 spectators during Long March 5 and 7 liftoffs. The centre, operational since 2016, is China's only coastal spaceport (just 19° north latitude, ideal for launches). Check the launch schedule before going.

⏱ 90 min
3

Beyond Longlou when coming from Wenchang, the stage town, you will find the Tongguling Nature Reserve, home to the “first peak of eastern Hainan”, an ancient volcano that collapsed into the sea. Trails through subtropical forest, plunging views over the bays of the east coast. The Wenchang rock park, nearby, displays spectacular marine erosion formations along 2 km of coastline. Bonus: it is one of the eight recommended sites for watching the official launches.

⏱ 90 min
4

Downtown, detour to Wenchang Confucius Temple, founded under the Song and rebuilt in 1042, one of the best-preserved in southern China. You'll find red courtyards, lion statues, and red lacquered wooden gates that still host spring examination rituals today. Wenchang historically produced more imperial officials than any other Hainan city.

⏱ 60 min

Activities

Lunch on Wenchang chicken in its home village. The chicken is free-range raised in coconut groves, fed on fermented coconut and small fish - hence its particularly rich meat. A typical Tianci district restaurant (part of Wenchang) has served the dish for three generations.

⏱ 90 min

If luck is with you, witness a rocket launch from Yaoguang platform. A Long March 5 liftoff - roughly 869 tonnes at lift-off - produces a deep rumble felt for several kilometres. White light, orange trail, vibrations in your chest according to eyewitnesses. Rare spectacle in China, not to be missed if your visit coincides with a launch.

⏱ 60 min

Xinglong

🛏️ Suggested base : Xinglong
★★☆☆☆
1

Welcome to Boao, 60 km south-east of Wenchang, which in two decades has gone from a small fishing village to the seat of the Boao Forum for Asia - the “Asian Davos” founded in 2001. Boao is a stop along the way: Xinglong, the stage town, is still a long way down the road. You will discover the conference centre, which welcomes the region's heads of state every spring. The peninsula where three rivers (Wanquan, Jiuqu, Longgun) meet before flowing into the sea offers a panorama unique in China.

⏱ 90 min
2

Walk along Yudai Beach (“jade belt”), 5 km from Boao centre, which separates the river estuary from the open sea. It's one of the world's longest narrow sand strips - 8 km long and just a few metres wide. Clear water, palm trees, few people on weekdays. The sunset walk between river and sea is stunning.

⏱ 60 min
3

Head for Xinglong, a former village created in the 1950s-70s to take in overseas Chinese repatriated from Indonesia, Vietnam, Malaysia and Burma. Today you will still hear Javanese, Vietnamese and Malay spoken in some families. The Tropical Botanical Garden, founded in 1957, is home to more than 1,200 plant species of Southeast Asian origin.

⏱ 90 min
4

Head to Xinglong coffee village, planted in the 1950s by Indonesian repatriates, where Hainanese arabica robusta is cultivated. You'll see old artisans still roasting over wood fires in earthen ovens. Coffee is served very strong, sweet, sometimes with butter - a local tradition derived from Javanese cafés. Small tasting and beans to take home.

⏱ 60 min

Activities

Coffee roasting workshop in Xinglong, at a family farm. You'll see the complete process: sorting green beans, roasting over open fire, hand grinding, Singapore-style tasting (arm extended, sock filter). Allow about 90 minutes. Leave with a bag of your own roast.

⏱ 90 min

Try the Malay-Chinese meal in Xinglong. On the menu: Hainanese nasi lemak, satay, sambal, coconut curry rice. A legacy of the returnees of the 1960s. A well-chilled Tsingtao beer to go with it.

⏱ 90 min

Wanning

🛏️ Suggested base : Xinglong · 23 km
★★☆☆☆
1

Welcome to Wanning, China's surf capital. You arrive at Riyue Bay (“Sun-Moon”), a Chinese stop on the World Surf League Qualifying Series - 2025 edition, 11-17 December, over 160 surfers from more than ten countries. Consistent waves, sandy bottom, local surf school. Waves are best November through March, when the north-east swell is consistent.

⏱ 60 min
2

Thirty kilometers north, you'll discover Shimei Bay ("stone beauty"), which draws a perfect crescent of white sand for 9 km. It's one of the island's most beautiful beaches, still relatively unspoiled. Several luxury hotels (Banyan Tree, Le Méridien) have placed their villas here, but beach access remains public. Turquoise water as far as the eye can see, ideal for a morning at the beach before sightseeing.

⏱ 120 min
3

Nanwan Monkey Island, far to the south (allow a day round trip from Wanning, the stage town), is home to about 1,500 Chinese macaques. You reach it by a 2,138 m cable car over the sea, inaugurated in 1996, which was for a long time the longest of its kind in China. The monkeys are semi-wild, very curious, even aggressive if you wave food around. Do not wear sunglasses on your head, they steal them. A scientific reserve since 1965.

⏱ 120 min

Activities

Take a surf lesson at Riyue Bay (2 hours, board and wetsuit provided). Several certified schools on the beach, English-speaking instructors. Beginner to intermediate level - waves break gently, perfect for learning to stand up. The best session for you is 7:30 am, before wind and crowds.

⏱ 120 min

Late afternoon, walk along Shimei Bay seawall at sunset. Light shifts from gold to pink in fifteen minutes, the sky reflects in the shallow water. A few small grilled fish restaurants set up tables on the sand - order local grouper with salt and lime.

⏱ 90 min

Sanya

🛏️ Suggested base : Sanya
★★★★☆
1

Before Sanya, stop in Lingshui for Xincun Bay, about 1 hour's drive from Sanya, the stage town. There you will see the large floating village of the Tanka - a fishing people who have lived on the water for 700 years, descended from the coastal migrants of the Song dynasty. The houses sit on bamboo rafts moored in neighbourhoods. You get there by boat, visit fish farms and have lunch with a local family. It remains one of the last authentic Tanka communities in China.

⏱ 120 min
2

Sanya, the island's premier beach resort, awaits you. First discovery: Yalong Bay, considered the island's most beautiful beach - 7 km of fine white sand, translucent turquoise water up to 30 metres from shore. Dubbed “China's first bay.” Luxury hotels line their villas along the waterfront, but the beach is free access. Sea at 26°C in winter, ideal for swimming.

⏱ 90 min
3

Stroll along Sanya Bay, the urban beach in the heart of the city. 22 km of coastline lined with coconut palms, dotted with small family beaches. More lively than Yalong Bay, more accessible. Spectacular sunset over the South China Sea - light shifts from orange to mauve quickly. Drink huts and bars on the sand.

⏱ 60 min

Sanya

🛏️ Suggested base : Sanya
★★★★☆
1

Head south-west of Sanya to the Nanshan Cultural Park (about 40 km from Sanya, a 40-minute drive), founded in 1988 for the 2,000 years of Buddhism in China. Spread over 30 km², you will find several Tang-style temples, gardens and statues. The Guanyin of Nanshan statue, standing on an artificial islet linked to the coast by a 280 m causeway, is 108 metres high - one of the tallest statues in the world. It was inaugurated on 24 April 2005, after 6 years of construction, in the presence of 108 monks from all over Asia.

⏱ 120 min
2

The Guanyin has three faces: one looks inland to bless China, the other two look to sea to protect the world. One face holds a sutra, another a rosary, the third a lotus. Mixed metal structure coated with anti-salt fluoropolymer, roughly 2,600 tonnes total. Inside, in Yuantong Hall, you'll find a Guanyin statue in gold and jade inlaid with precious stones (the “small” Guanyin set with 100 kg of gold and 120 carats of diamonds). You can climb to the goddess's feet for the view.

⏱ 60 min
3

Twenty kilometres away, you arrive at Tianya Haijiao (“Edge of Sky, Corner of Sea”), a site that owes its name to large rocks inscribed with Song dynasty calligraphy. Under the Tang, it was an exile site for disgraced officials - they said it was “the end of the world.” Chinese couples come en masse to bless their love, it's become an iconic wedding site in southern China.

⏱ 90 min
4

Luhuitou Park ("the deer turns back"), on the eponymous hill, offers the best panoramic view of Sanya Bay. The bronze deer statue, erected in 1987, stands 12 m high. Li legend tells of a hunter who chased a deer to the cliff edge - the animal stopped, turned around and transformed into a young girl, who became his wife. It's the mythical origin of the Li people.

⏱ 60 min

Activities

Vegetarian meal at Nanshan Temple, famous throughout China. Refined Buddhist cuisine, no garlic or onion, based on tofu, mushrooms, vegetables and meat imitations. Served in a large quiet hall with sea views. Allow 80-150 yuan per person, it's one of Asia's finest vegetarian meals.

⏱ 90 min

Relaxing evening at a Yalong Bay hotel spa. Traditional Hainanese massage with coconut oil and local aloe, 90 minutes for 400-600 yuan depending on hotels. Most resorts accept non-guests for their spas. Take advantage to recover after this big cultural day.

⏱ 90 min

Sanya

🛏️ Suggested base : Sanya
★★★★☆
1

Head to Wuzhizhou Island, 30 km east of Sanya. Despite its name often mistranslated as “Monkey Island,” there are no macaques here - common confusion with Nanwan, don't make that mistake. Wuzhizhou is one of the island's best diving and snorkelling spots. Visibility up to 25 m in clear weather, corals, clownfish, turtles. The island covers 1.5 km², ferry from Houhai port (10-minute crossing).

⏱ 240 min
2

Head to Haitang Bay, north-east of Sanya, which became the island's new chic bay since 2010. You'll see the Atlantis Sanya complex (opened 2018), with its giant aquarium, water park, and 1,314 rooms. Beach stretching 22 km, still wild in places - but urbanisation is advancing quickly. Worth seeing for the contrast between preserved nature and XXL hotels.

⏱ 90 min
3

If you still have energy late in the day, climb Phoenix Mountain for the panoramic view of the three bays (Yalong, Haitang and Sanya Bay). Cable car in 8 minutes, observation platform at 480 m. It's an opportunity to locate all the beaches you've seen these two days, and see the South China Sea at a glance.

⏱ 60 min

Activities

Snorkelling or introductory dive at Wuzhizhou. Several PADI-certified centres on the island, complete equipment. Diving at -8/-12 m, 30 minutes underwater, allow 600-800 yuan with a dedicated instructor. Water at 25°C even in winter, so no need for a thick wetsuit.

⏱ 180 min

Duty-free shopping at Sanya International Duty-Free. Hainan is China's only total free port, and mainland Chinese fly in by the planeload to buy cosmetics, perfumes, watches, alcohol. Annual quotas of 100,000 yuan duty-free per person. For foreigners, it's less advantageous than Hong Kong, but simpler if you're already here.

⏱ 120 min

Wuzhishan

🛏️ Suggested base : Wuzhishan
★★★★★
1

Head for Wuzhishan, the “Five Finger Mountain”, sacred mountain of the Li, the island's aboriginal people, present here for more than 3,000 years. The reserve lies about 35 km from the town of Wuzhishan, a 1-hour drive. You will cross a primary tropical forest, in a national reserve opened in 1985. The main peak rises to 1,867 m. Marked hiking trail, rare wildlife.

⏱ 180 min
2

Shuiman village, nestled in the valley at 600 m altitude, is a Li cultural centre. You'll see traditional “upturned boat-shaped” houses (chuanxing wu), thatched roofs. A few rare Li grandmothers still wear ancestral facial tattoos - a practice banned since 1949, dying out with them. The last tattooed generation is now 80-95 years old.

⏱ 90 min
3

In the same area as Shuiman, the village of Maona offers an immersion in everyday Li life: brocade weaving, drying of medicinal herbs, rearing of black-footed pigs. Li brocade (Li jin) has 3,000 years of history. Inscribed in 2009 on the UNESCO List of Intangible Heritage in Need of Urgent Safeguarding (and not on the Representative List), it has since appeared on the catwalk at Paris Fashion Week in 2024 and 2025 thanks to the Dalishen and Gangong bird motifs.

⏱ 60 min
4

Rise early for the Yahu terraced rice fields, in Maoyang township, considered Hainan's most beautiful. They climb the mountain slopes, water mirrors in spring, golden waves in autumn. More modest than Yuanyang's in Yunnan, but much more accessible and less crowded. Morning light is finest.

⏱ 60 min

Activities

Li brocade weaving lesson with a local artisan in Maona or Shuiman. You learn the basics - foot loom, shuttle, cotton threads dyed with bark and roots. Li women traditionally weave sitting on the ground. Allow 2-3 hours to make a small sample (bracelet or bookmark) that you'll take home. Fair rates, around 200-300 yuan per session.

⏱ 180 min

Li meal in a Shuiman family: chicken with ants (the “ant chicken” is a local breed), pandanus leaf rice, black Shanlan rice wine (sacred wine served in bamboo), wild forest vegetables. Taste the “jianlan jiu,” sweet cardamom wine. A cuisine very different from mainland China.

⏱ 120 min

Haikou

🛏️ Suggested base : Haikou
★★★☆☆
1

Return to Haikou in two stages. First, 80 km by road from Wuzhishan to Sanya HSR station (about 90 minutes), as Wuzhishan isn't served by the circular rail line. Then a 90-minute express train to Haikou East, along the south-east coast. The complete Hainan loop totals 653 km - the world's only circular high-speed railway, western section completed in 2015. Views of rice paddies and coconut groves from the carriage.

⏱ 60 min
2

Upon arrival, end your stay with a stop at Haikou International Duty-Free Shopping Complex. It's the world's largest duty-free store since opening in 2022 - 280,000 m², over 800 international brands. For foreigners, it's a chance to buy alcohol, perfumes and cosmetics tax-free before the return flight.

⏱ 120 min
3

Before Meilan airport (25 minutes from downtown), take a final walk through Qilou arcade street, grab a last Hainan coffee on a terrace. Wenchang chicken rice, noodle soup, green mango with chilli - the classics for a last meal. Meilan airport has direct connections to most major Chinese cities, plus Hong Kong, Singapore, Bangkok and Seoul.

⏱ 60 min

Activities

If your flight leaves in the evening, stop by Mission Hills Haikou, a golf complex with its ten courses designed by Jack Nicklaus, Greg Norman, Annika Sörenstam and others. Green fees of 600-1,200 yuan on weekdays, booking required. Spa and hot springs on site for those who do not play.

⏱ 180 min
