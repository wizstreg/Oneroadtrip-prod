Trip info

✨ Highlights▾
  • Palatul Cultural in Arad: blend of neoclassical, Gothic and Renaissance styles, concert hall where George Enescu performed
  • Peștera Scărișoara: 75,000 m³ underground glacier in the Apuseni, at 1,165 m altitude
  • Oradea: Pasaj Vulturul Negru from 1908, Transylvania's largest Secession passage
  • Băile Felix: thermal waters between 20 and 49 °C, Nymphaea lotus thermalis water lilies in the lakes
☀️ Weather▾

Best time: May-June and September-October, temperatures 18-26°C in the plains, 15-20°C in the mountains. Avoid July-August (scorching heat in Arad and Oradea, frequent storms in the Apuseni) and December-March (harsh cold, difficult cave access, snow on mountain roads). Vineyards and caves are best visited in spring and early autumn.

📋 Notes▾

Winding mountain roads between Scărișoara and Padiș: bring a suitable vehicle and check track conditions before departure. Some caves close on Mondays.

Crișana & Apuseni: Caves, Vineyards & Art Nouveau, 8 days

Arad

★★★☆☆
1

Look up beneath the dome of Arad's Palatul Cultural: the fresco depicts the solar system and Halley's comet, gilded with gold leaf. Built between 1911 and 1913 by local architect Szántay Lajos, this palace blends neoclassical, Gothic and Italian Renaissance styles. Richard Strauss, Béla Bartók and George Enescu all performed here. Closed Monday mornings.

⏱ 60 min
2

Piața Avram Iancu opens wide, lined with pastel-hued Art Nouveau façades. Take time to spot the red-brick Administrative Palace and the neo-Gothic church. The covered market, a stone's throw away, sells fresh langos for a few lei.

⏱ 45 min
3

If you've got another hour, push on to the Orthodox Cathedral of St John the Baptist. Its massive proportions are surprising in a city this size. Construction lasted from 1862 to 1865, funded by the Romanian community.

⏱ 30 min

Siria

★★★☆☆
1

The climb to Cetatea Șiria takes 20 minutes—steep but short. At the top, the 13th-century ruins dominate the entire Arad plain. You can still make out a western wall over 20 meters high and the remains of a Romanesque keep. Destroyed by the Habsburgs in 1784, the site retains a raw beauty. Proper footwear essential.

⏱ 60 min

Activities

Head back down to the Miniș-Măderat vineyards for a tasting. This wine region, documented as far back as 1212, stretches 40 km along the foothills of the Zarand mountains. Try the Fetească Neagră, a native varietal, at Balla Géza or Crama Daiconi. Expect to pay 15-20 lei for a tasting.

⏱ 60 min
🚗Drive to next stop: 25 km · 35min

Cetatea Soimos

★★★★☆
1

Quirky, the name—Cetatea Șoimoș takes its name from the medieval practice of breeding hunting falcons. But the view from these 13th-century ruins, perched above the Mureș, is anything but quirky. Documented in 1278, the fortress belonged to Iancu de Hunedoara, then served as a garrison for Mihai Viteazul in 1599-1600. Twenty-minute climb, steep slope. Bring water.

⏱ 60 min
🚗Drive to next stop: 3 km · 5min

Radna

★★★☆☆
1

You hear prayers in Romanian, Hungarian and German under the same nave: the Basilica Maria Radna has united three communities for centuries. Elevated to Basilica Minor status by John Paul II in 1992, it houses a miraculous icon dating from 1668 and a museum of over 2,500 painted ex-votos. The Carrara marble altar alone is worth the detour. Free entry.

⏱ 45 min
🚗Drive to next stop: 45 km · 50min

Savarsin

★★☆☆☆
1

Purchased in 1943 by King Mihai, confiscated by the communists, returned in 2001: Castelul Săvârșin carries Romania's turbulent history in its walls. The castle itself is not open to visitors—it's the royal family's private residence—but the Royal Park opens from May to October, Friday through Sunday. The small 18th-century pavilion and the wisteria-lined avenue justify the stop.

⏱ 60 min
🚗Drive to next stop: 90 km · 2h

Garda De Sus

★★☆☆☆
1

Ice in the middle of summer: that's the promise of Peștera Scărișoara. Descend 48 meters via a metal staircase into a gaping chasm, and discover an underground glacier of roughly 75,000 m³, formed thousands of years ago. The cave extends 720 m at an altitude of 1,165 m. Temperature around 0 °C at the bottom—bring a sweater even in August.

⏱ 60 min

Activities

You can combine the visit with a hike in the Ordâncușa valley, accessible on foot from the parking lot. The gorges offer a narrow limestone landscape, crossed by a road. Count on 1h30 round trip, easy difficulty.

⏱ 90 min
🚗Drive to next stop: 25 km · 1h

Padis

★★★★☆
1

The portal of Cetățile Ponorului measures 74 meters high—it looks like the entrance to a Gothic cathedral carved from rock. This is Romania's largest karst cave, in the heart of the Padiș plateau. Inside, an underground stream disappears into a deep lake. The full circuit from Cabana Padiș takes 3 to 4 hours. Rocky terrain, crampons recommended after rain.

⏱ 180 min

Activities

Extend the day with the Cheile Galbenei circuit if your legs can handle it. This wooded canyon follows a torrent and opens onto Izbucul Galbenei. Add 2 hours to the program. Red triangle blazes from the cabin.

⏱ 120 min
🚗Drive to next stop: 15 km · 45min

Cascada Bohodei

★★★★☆
1

At 80 meters, Cascada Bohodei is the highest in the Apuseni. It doesn't fall in a single drop: the water tumbles down the rock in several tiers, making it less spectacular from below but impressive from the ridge. Warning: the final ascent requires a minimum of ease on exposed terrain (grade 2A). Count on 1 hour from the Valea Aleului parking lot.

⏱ 120 min
🚗Drive to next stop: 35 km · 1h

Chiscau

★★☆☆☆
1

Discovered by chance in 1975 during quarry blasting, Peștera Urșilor owes its name to the 140 cave bear skeletons trapped here roughly 15,000 years ago. Four chambers follow one another—the Candles Hall, the Spaghetti Hall, the Emil Racoviță Hall and the Bones Hall. Constant temperature of 10 °C. Guided tour of 45 minutes, Tuesday to Sunday.

⏱ 60 min
🚗Drive to next stop: 40 km · 1h10

Stana De Vale

★★★★☆
1

Perched at 1,100 m in the Vlădeasa massif, Stâna de Vale moves at a slow pace. The air is charged with negative ions, the waters are ferruginous, and hiking trails head off in all directions. The Izvorul Minunilor (Fountain of Miracles), a 30-minute walk away, is surrounded by local legends about its healing properties. No ATM on site, bring cash.

⏱ 60 min

Activities

Take the marked trail to Vârf Piatra Calului for a panorama over the entire range. Count on 3 hours round trip, medium difficulty. The blazes (blue stripe) are reliable.

⏱ 180 min
🚗Drive to next stop: 30 km · 55min

Remetea

★★☆☆☆
1

The portal of Peștera Meziad measures 16 meters high and 10 wide—you enter as into an underground Gothic cathedral. With 4,750 m of development over four levels, it's one of Romania's longest developed caves. Emil Racoviță studied it in 1921. The protected bat colony is impressive. Guided tour of one hour, variable hours depending on the season.

⏱ 60 min

Activities

On the road to Meziad, stop in front of the centuries-old oak of Remetea, a pedunculate oak reputed to be around 400 years old, the last survivor of a forest that once linked the two villages. It reaches about 25 m high. Photo mandatory.

⏱ 15 min
🚗Drive to next stop: 35 km · 50min

Vadu Crisului

★★☆☆☆
1

Discovered by dynamiting in 1903, Peștera Vadu Crișului is traversed by an underground stream that forms a 9-meter waterfall as it exits. The 680 m guided route shows formations with imaginative names: Mormântul lui Mahomed, Vulturul Alb, Eva. You can get there on foot from the Peștera train halt, on the Cluj-Oradea line. Developed as early as 1905, it remains one of the most visited caves in Bihor.

⏱ 60 min

Activities

Go down to admire Cascada Vadu Crișului from the base. A steep path leads there; the niche carved into the limestone lets you feel the force of the water. Declared a nature reserve in 1955 along with the gorge.

⏱ 20 min
🚗Drive to next stop: 55 km · 1h

Oradea

★★★☆☆
1

The Palatul Vulturul Negru overshadows everything you've seen. This Y-shaped passage, inspired by Milan's Galleria Vittorio Emanuele, dates from 1908. Its 1907 stained-glass window depicting a black eagle casts colored reflections when the sun enters. It's the largest Art Nouveau commercial complex in Transylvania. Today, cafés and bars occupy the ground floor. Free, open.

⏱ 45 min
2

Walk around Piața Unirii on foot: each façade tells a different century. Baroque, Art Nouveau, neoclassical coexist over 200 meters. Oradea has 89 listed Art Nouveau buildings—the highest concentration in Romania. The City Hall Palace, with its clock tower, closes the vista.

⏱ 45 min
3

The Cetatea Oradea, a pentagonal fortress, now hosts exhibitions and festivals. The bastioned enclosure dates from the 16th century, but the site has been fortified for much longer. In summer, an open-air bar sets up in the courtyard. Free entry to the courtyard, paid for exhibitions.

⏱ 60 min
4

Cross the bridge to reach the Palatul Baroc (Episcopal Palace), nicknamed "the palace with 365 windows." Built in the 18th century, it houses a museum and interior courtyards. The style recalls imperial Vienna, not deep Romania. Open Tuesday to Sunday.

⏱ 45 min

Baile Felix

★★★☆☆
1

The thermal lakes of Băile Felix host a rare tenant: the water lily Nymphaea lotus thermalis, a subtropical species that survived from the Tertiary era. The water emerges between 20 and 49 °C depending on the spring, rich in calcium, sodium and bicarbonates. Monk Felix Helcher founded the first baths between 1711 and 1721. Outdoor pool entry around 30-50 lei depending on the complex.

⏱ 120 min

Activities

If you're traveling with children, Nymphaea Aqua Park offers slides, wave pools and a wellness area. Plan at least 3 hours. Open year-round.

⏱ 180 min
🚗Drive to next stop: 35 km · 40min

Salonta

★★★☆☆
1

In Salonta, Romanians and Hungarians have coexisted for centuries, and it shows. The Turnul Ciunt (Truncated Tower), built between 1606 and 1630, serves as an observation tower and today houses the Arany János Memorial Museum, dedicated to the great Hungarian poet born here in 1817. Four floors, 22 meters high. The collection was renovated in 2018 with support from Budapest's Petőfi Museum.

⏱ 45 min

Activities

If multiculturalism interests you, cross the Romanian quarter to see the small village museum documenting local peasant life. Salonta also has a bison farm, accessible to groups by reservation.

⏱ 30 min
