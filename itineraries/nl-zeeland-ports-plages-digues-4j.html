Middelburg

🛏️ Suggested base : Veere · 7 km
★★★★☆
1

It is hard to imagine a more theatrical introduction than Middelburg’s Stadhuis. Behind the Gothic facade, the interior was rebuilt after the fire of 1940, so its historic appearance does not tell the whole story. Step back onto the Markt to admire the entire facade, then walk along the facade on Lange Noordstraat.

⏱ 30 min
2

Burning ships cover the walls of the Zeeuws Museum. Its six tapestries depict the people of Zeeland fighting the Spanish; they were made between 1593 and 1604. Start with these scenes before exploring the other collections, and plan your visit for after 11 a.m.: the museum is normally closed on Mondays.

⏱ 75 min
3

Climb the steps of Lange Jan to take in the sweep of Walcheren. The abbey tower rises above Middelburg, with the surrounding countryside visible on clear days. The 207 steps call for more than a quick photo stop: save the climb for good visibility and be prepared to pay by card.

Veere

🛏️ Suggested base : Veere
★★★☆☆
1

Trade with Scotland left Veere with houses rather than just archives: the Schotse Huizen house part of Museum Veere. The visit continues at the Stadhuis on the Markt. Keep the same ticket for both locations; it covers both buildings, whose upper floors have no elevator access.

⏱ 75 min
2

Enormous for such a small town, Veere’s Grote Kerk was nevertheless never completed. Projections and stories now bring new life to its nave. In spring and fall, the church generally opens Thursday through Sunday; the tower may close late in the season to protect the bats that hibernate there. Check opening hours before climbing.

Domburg

🛏️ Suggested base : Vlissingen · 14 km
★★★★☆
1

The Marie Tak van Poortvliet Museum showcases the paintings behind Domburg’s artistic reputation. Toorop and Mondrian frequented this resort, but the works on display vary with the exhibition schedule. From September 2026 to June 2027, construction work means the museum welcomes visitors at Noordstraat 13; its return to Ooststraat 10a is planned afterward.

⏱ 45 min

Activities

Head down to Domburg Strand after the museum to rediscover the simple lines of the shoreline. The rows of wooden posts extending into the sea serve as breakwaters; they also create long perspectives across the sand. Keep your distance from the posts as you walk, and let the wind guide when you turn back, without necessarily planning on a swim.

Westkapelle

🛏️ Suggested base : Vlissingen · 13 km
★★★☆☆
1

If you are looking for a lighthouse by the water, Westkapelle’s Hoge Licht will surprise you: it stands on the village side. This former church tower still guides boats. Choose a clear day for the climb; access to the panoramic view may be closed during storms or bad weather.

⏱ 30 min
2

In 1944, bombing of the dike let the sea flood into Westkapelle; the Polderhuismuseum recounts the human consequences. Personal accounts and photographs connect this history to work on the dikes. Keep your phone handy for content accessed through QR codes, then view the coast through the lens of this story.

Vlissingen

🛏️ Suggested base : Vlissingen
★★★☆☆
1

To understand what passes offshore, the Maritiem Muzeeum Zeeland is worth a stop in Vlissingen. Objects recovered from shipwrecks tell the story of life aboard, including bottles that still contain their wine. Pick up the audio guide included with admission and leave large bags in the coatroom; unlike the rest of the museum, the historic tower is not wheelchair accessible.

⏱ 75 min

Activities

Walk along Boulevard De Ruyter in Vlissingen to watch ships head into the estuary. Cargo ships pass close enough to shore to give the outdoor seating areas the feel of observation posts. Continue on foot toward Boulevard Bankert; avoid trying to drive through, as traffic is restricted during the season.

Yerseke

🛏️ Suggested base : Zierikzee · 20 km
★★★☆☆
1

Yerseke’s oyster ponds look like small, tranquil pools, but they are part of an industry that is still very much alive. Connected to the Oosterschelde, they support the work of oyster farmers and follow the rhythm of the tides. Come at low tide: the ponds partially drain, revealing the oysters.

⏱ 45 min

Activities

Settle in at the Oesterij in Yerseke to compare the oysters on offer, overlooking the ponds and the Oosterschelde. The tasting may include oysters from different places: ask which come from Zeeland if you want to try locally produced oysters. Arrive before the lunchtime rush; the tasting counter does not take table reservations.

Goes

🛏️ Suggested base : Zierikzee · 16 km
★★★☆☆
1

Same name, different church: in Goes, the Grote of Maria Magdalenakerk stands near another Maria Magdalenakerk. Choose the large Protestant church for this stop, with its chapels and organ. From April through October, plan your visit Tuesday through Saturday between 1 p.m. and 4 p.m., except during private events.

⏱ 35 min
2

Facades are reflected between the boats in Stadshaven Goes. The names Bierkade and Turfkade recall the former beer and peat trades; the harbor tells the city’s story without a monumental sign.

Zierikzee

🛏️ Suggested base : Zierikzee
★★★★☆
1

Known as the “Dikke Toren,” or “fat tower,” the Sint-Lievensmonstertoren is Zierikzee’s most recognizable landmark. Its panoramic view unfolds 62 meters above the city. Arrive at least one hour before closing to make the climb; the ground-floor exhibition is free to visit.

⏱ 45 min
2

Follow Zierikzee’s quays to the Zuidhavenpoort and Noordhavenpoort. These medieval gates protected access from the harbor; between them, a bascule bridge spans the old harbor entrance. Continue your walk along the Nieuwe Haven to see the historic boats.

Brouwershaven

🛏️ Suggested base : Middelburg · 33 km
★★☆☆☆
1

Too large for this small harbor town? Brouwershaven’s Sint-Nicolaaskerk gives that impression as soon as you reach the forecourt. Its scale and Renaissance organ case recall a history more ambitious than the present-day town suggests. Interior access depends on opening times and events; plan primarily to explore the exterior, without counting on it always being open.

⏱ 30 min
2

Brouwershaven’s harbor reaches almost to the tables in the town center, with the Stadhuis as a backdrop. The former town hall took on a new use after the municipal reorganization; its Renaissance facade stands out on the square. Look for the statue of Justice above the door; the town hall’s interior is closed to the public.

Neeltje Jans

🛏️ Suggested base : Middelburg · 15 km
★★★★☆
1

In 1953, the sea overwhelmed the dikes; at Deltapark Neeltje Jans, the Delta Experience puts this disaster at the heart of your journey. Films and exhibitions explain the structures built afterward, with a detour into the Oosterschelde ecosystem. Book your date using the park’s calendar: the surrounding natural areas are separate from this paid attraction.

⏱ 150 min
2

If you want to see the Oosterscheldekering without visiting all the attractions, head to the shore near the Topshuis. Beneath the open gates, the tide continues to flow: the storm surge barrier protects the estuary without permanently cutting it off from the sea. The structure’s interior is closed to the public; use the marked outdoor access routes to view it.

⏱ 30 min

Activities

Sand crunches underfoot in the dunes of Neeltje Jans. After construction of the storm surge barrier, the wind shaped a landscape of marram grass and sea buckthorn on this former construction island. Choose a short loop from the marked routes and stay on the paths, especially near areas reserved for birds.

⏱ 45 min
