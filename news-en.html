28 July 2026

New feature: daily loops

OneRoadTrip keeps improving the app. Until now, a road trip was either linear (from point A to point Z) or a full loop (back to the starting point). The app now also handles hotel stays of several nights in the same place.

In practice, your daily loops appear on the map in distinct colors. For example: you sleep in Nice, visit three nearby towns and return to Nice, that route shows in blue. The next day, you leave Nice for three new towns before coming back for the night, that second route shows in red, and so on.

This makes it easy to tell each sightseeing day apart when you stay several nights in the same place. And in your travel guides, you can now select a specific day without displaying the other stops.

How OneRoadTrip uses AI, responsibly

A behind-the-scenes look at how OneRoadTrip builds its itineraries. AI writes the first draft based on real web sources. Every itinerary is then reviewed, corrected and validated through multiple passes before publication.

AI alone cannot yet produce an error-free itinerary. The combination of AI generation and rigorous human verification is what makes OneRoadTrip content reliable.

16 August 2025

How OneRoadTrip began

Ahead of his trip to Sri Lanka, Marc Sorci searches online for a simple organizer to replace his Excel spreadsheets: daily stages, visits and their duration, travel routes, hotels and photos.

Unable to find a tool offering all of this, he decides to build his own.
