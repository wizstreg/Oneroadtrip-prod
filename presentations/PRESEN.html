1What is OneRoadTrip?

OneRoadTrip is a free platform for building travel itineraries. It lets you browse, customise and create complete itineraries with an interactive map, route calculation, detailed descriptions of every stop and hotel integration.

Where many tools start from a blank page, ORT offers a catalogue of 1,150 ready-made itineraries covering 105 countries and 8,859 points of interest, available in 7 languages (French, English, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, German, Dutch).

Each itinerary is built from cross-checked sources, reviewed, then validated by the team before publication. Its validation date is displayed on the page.

2How does it work?

For the traveller

  • Browse the itinerary catalogue (as a list or on a map) by country, duration or theme
  • View each stop with its detailed visits, photos and practical tips
  • Customise the itinerary: add, remove or reorder stops and visits
  • See everything on an interactive map with distances and travel times
  • Book hotels, flights, cars and activities directly from the itinerary
  • Import your bookings into the saved trip
  • Export as a travel journal and share with loved ones
  • View the trip offline

5 ways to find or create an itinerary

By criteria

Filter the catalogue of 1,150 ready-made itineraries by country, duration, theme or distance.

Between two points

Enter a departure and an arrival point: the tool suggests the best places along the way.

From places

Choose your stops one by one from the 8,859 rated places, or add a place that is not yet on the site.

On the world map

Explore the whole catalogue on a globe and let yourself be tempted.

With Lilia

Describe what you feel like doing to the assistant, who points you to the right itineraries.

3Key features

Editorial content

Every stop comes with written text: visits to make, activities, practical tips, recommended duration. Travel guide content, not a Google listing.

Dated verification

A badge on each itinerary shows the date of its last validation. A button lets you report an error in one click.

Carbon score

Each itinerary displays a carbon score assessing its environmental impact based on distances, transport and number of stops.

Multilingual

Interface and itineraries in 7 languages (FR, EN, ES, IT, PT, DE, NL). Every translation is checked against the French original.

Integrated bookings

Hotels, activities, cars and flights can be booked from the itinerary through Booking.com, Viator, Trip.com and Qeeq. The service remains free for the traveller.

Artificial intelligence

Mobile AI guide (photo → the place's story), image translation, detailed AI reviews of places.
